## How to Make an Oouuh AI Video

1. **Add the two people.** Upload a clear portrait for the ukulele player and another for the singer. If you want to use one photo together, turn on Original mode and choose the duo-photo option.
2. **Pick the environment and settings.** Choose the kitchen, living room, beach or rooftop. Keep Original mode off for the original Oouuh soundtrack, or turn it on for a new song.
3. **Give your original song a subject.** This step is optional and applies to Original mode. Add a name, occasion or shared memory. You can request a singing language, or leave the topic empty for an AI-chosen subject.
4. **Review the cost and create.** Sign in and check the credits or preview eligibility shown in the creator. Follow the submitted video in [My videos](https://oouuhai.com/settings/works), which requires sign-in.
5. **Watch and download.** Paid or unlocked videos include the complete MP4 with sound. Save it before its displayed expiry date, then upload it to your chosen social platform.

Photos can be **JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, up to 20 MB each**. HEIC files are converted automatically. Clear faces and good lighting help; use photos with the permission of the people shown.

## OOUUH AI FAQ

### Do I need a CapCut template or an app?

No. Use the [OOUUH AI creator](https://oouuhai.com/#create) in your browser. Add your photos and choose the settings; no filmed performance or audio recording is needed.

### Can I use one photo of us together?

Yes, in Original mode. Original Motion needs two separate portraits. With a duo photo in Original mode, the two roles are assigned automatically and stay fixed during the performance.

### Will it use our real voices?

Photos do not clone your voices. Original Motion keeps the reference audio; Original mode creates AI vocals and new ukulele music.

### Will the movements and faces match exactly?

The photos guide the performers' appearance, and the reference guides Original Motion. AI-generated faces, hands, timing and lip-sync can vary. The examples show actual outputs, not a guarantee of an identical result with other photos.

### Can I choose the song's language?

In Original mode, request a language in the topic. Otherwise, the topic's main language guides the lyrics, followed by the site language when the topic gives no clear language. Original Motion keeps its fixed soundtrack. Language accuracy can vary.

### Can I close the page while it generates?

Yes, once your video has been submitted. Return to the same account and check [My videos](https://oouuhai.com/settings/works). Processing time depends on the queue and settings.

### Can I download and share the video?

Paid and unlocked videos include a full MP4 without our preview watermark. Download it and upload it yourself to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts or your preferred platform. Follow the platform's AI-content rules. Downloading does not grant music, likeness or commercial-use rights you do not already have.

### How long can I access my video?

Locked free results are kept for 7 days. Paid videos are kept for 30 days from completion; unlocked free videos are kept for 30 days from unlocking. Download before the expiry shown on your work. See the [Privacy Policy](https://oouuhai.com/privacy-policy) for photo processing and storage details.
